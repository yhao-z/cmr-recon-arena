Ground truth

OCMR test splits are scored against their own fully-sampled files. CMRxRecon prospective test splits carry no reference image and are scored against cmrxrecon23_test_fs (file names align because the pipeline drops the acquisition path segment).

Reproduce

Recompute the metrics:

python scripts/calc_metric.py \
  --recon-dir <dir> --gt-root <gt>

Rebuild this page:

python scripts/build_leaderboard.py

A static snapshot of the CSVs under reconstructions/.

Weights for every entry live under weights/<test set>/<method>.ckpt · rankings are computed on the fly in your browser, nothing is fetched.
